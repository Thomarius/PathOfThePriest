Objective

The Path of the Priest is a solo strategy game in which you must walk along a Path of 14 cards, made up of 8 True Masters and 6 Fake Masters. You must take the Apprentice (card 0) from the beginning of the Path to the end and connect with the Deity (end next to it).

Setup

1. Separate the Apprentice and Deity cards.
2. Shuffle the 14 remaining cards and form the Path by placing them from left to right.
3. Place the Apprentice card at the beginning of the Path (left).
4. Place the Deity card at the end of the Path (right).

How to play

The game is played in rounds in the following order:

Player Turn:

Choose any True Master within the Path and activate by resolving 1 of its effects.

Fake Masters Turn:

After the player’s turn, each Fake Master within the Path is activated from lowest to highest (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 11).

Begin a new round with the player’s turn.


End of the Game

You win if, at the end of the Fake Masters’ turn, the Apprentice is next to the Deity. You have become a priest!
You lose if the Apprentice is discarded from the game or if you cannot activate any True Master.


Clarifications

- You may activate the same True Master in consecutive rounds.
- A few True Masters have multiple effects. When activating them, choose only one of its effects and resolve it.
- If the movement from an effect cannot be carried out completely, you can’t resolve that effect.
- If you cannot activate any True Master, you lose immediately.
- When activating a Fake Master, resolve its top effect. If unable, the bottom effect offers an alternative. If neither effect can be resolved, ignore that Fake Master.
- The numbers of the effects are exact, unless preceded by the words “up to”.
Example: “Up to 2” means 1 or 2.
- No card can move back beyond the first position of the Path.
- No card can move beyond the Deity. You need to move the Apprentice exactly to the position on the left side of the Deity.
- No effect can affect the Deity.

Glossary

Adjacent: Immediately to its left or right (1 space away).
Advance: Move to the right.
Destroy: Take out of the Path and, therefore, from the game.
Swap: Exchange the indicated cards’ positions.
Send: Move directly to the indicated position.
Lowest: Card with the lowest number on its top left corner.
Move: Move in any direction (left or right).
Move back: Move to the left.


Card List

0 - APPRENTICE
(no card text)

1 - DOUBT
Fake Master
- Swap the 2 cards adjacent to this card.
- If unable, move this card 1 space towards the center of the path.

2 - GRUDGE
Fake Master
- Destroy the lowest adjacent card.

3 - FEAR
Fake Master
- Send the lowest adjacent card to the beginning of the path.

4 - LAZINESS
Fake Master
- Move back the lowest adjacent card 2 spaces.
- If unable, advance this card 1 space.

5 - EXERCISE
True Master
- Swap this card with a card that is up to 2 spaces away from this card.

6 - DISCORD
Fake Master
- Send the lowest adjacent card to the space before GRUDGE
- If it is GRUDGE, move GRUDGE 2 spaces towards the APPRENTICE, even if it moves beyond it.

7 - PHILOSOPHY
True Master
- Choose a card that is 3 spaces away from this card and move it 1 space.
- Choose a card that is 2 spaces away from this card and move it 2 space.
- Choose a card that is 1 spaces away from this card and move it 3 space.

8 - HERBALIST
True Master
- Move back any adjacent card 2 spaces.
- Advance any adjacent card 1 space.

9 - MEDITATION
True Master
- Swap this card with a card that is 3 spaces away from this card.

10 - PRAYER
True Master
- Swap this card with any adjacent card.

11 - ENVY
Fake Master
- Move the lowest adjacent card 2 spaces towards GRUDGE, even if it moves beyond it.
- If it is GRUDGE, move GRUDGE 2 spaces towards the APPRENTICE, even if it moves beyond it.

12 - PILGRIM
True Master
- Move any adjacent card 2 spaces.

13 - MAGIC
True Master
- Move back any adjacent card 3 spaces.
- Advance any adjacent card 2 spaces.

14 - ASTRAL BODY
True Master
- Swap this card with a card that is 4 spaces away from this card.

D - DIETY
(no card text)
